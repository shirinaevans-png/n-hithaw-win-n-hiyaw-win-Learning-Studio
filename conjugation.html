Scope and documentation

Plains examples are transcribed from the supplied Verb Rule Matrix and Noun Rule Matrix. Woodland examples come from the separately attributed TH-dialect material. Workbook examples whose prefix + stem + ending do not exactly reconstruct the written form are flagged and not silently corrected. This is a constrained, source-based morphological checker, not a universal conjugator.

Next linguistic coverage

To extend this safely to every dictionary lemma, record each stem class, allomorphy, conjunct and independent orders, VTI subtypes, VTA local/inverse relations, and obviation. A dictionary form alone is not sufficient to infer all inflections.
