Cree Place Names of Saskatchewan

Explore Cree names documented for northern Saskatchewan communities and Cree words reflected in some present-day place names. Search in English or Cree, then follow each entry to its published source.

A note on dialects and accuracy. The northern place-name source does not identify a distinct dialect for each entry. Forms have therefore not been assigned TH, Y, N or L based solely on geography. Cree-derived English names are shown separately from traditional place-name pairings. Spellings are reproduced from the cited publications; meanings and syllabics are not invented.

Research sources

LearnCree.ca — Northern Saskatchewan Place Names (Lac La Ronge Indian Band N-12 Education Program website). The list supplies place-name pairings for northern communities; dialect-specific attribution and English meanings are not established here.

Office of the Treaty Commissioner — Teaching Treaties in the Classroom, Appendix 7-11, p. 63. Indigenous-language origins of Saskatchewan place names; this page includes only entries expressly identified as Cree.

Cree Literacy Network — Barry Ahenakew: Place Names of the nêhiyawak (2023). A separate Plains Cree oral-history and mapping collection; it is not represented as entries in this initial list.

Saskatchewan Indigenous Cultural Centre — Traditional Place Names. Community-based place-name initiative for future verification and expansion.

This is an evolving educational research list, not an authoritative register of every Cree place name in Saskatchewan. Community language review is needed before assigning specific dialects or standardized syllabics.
