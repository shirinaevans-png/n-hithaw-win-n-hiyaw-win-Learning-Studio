Saskatchewan Cree Place Names

Traditional place-name pairings and names of Cree-language origin

Explore documented Cree names for Saskatchewan places. Search in English or Cree, inspect the published spelling, and follow the cited source. Where the source does not establish a specific dialect, no dialect is assigned.

Explore the interactive map ↗Download the research list (CSV)
Reading the evidence: Traditional place-name pairings identify names for a location. A Cree-derived English name identifies a linguistic origin, not necessarily an independently documented traditional name. Neither category establishes a TH, Y, N, or L dialect without a source.
