Who acts on whom?

Direct and inverse indicate how participants relate to the Cree person hierarchy. An inverse form is not necessarily an English passive. Imperatives are a separate verb-order category.

The forms here are short illustrative Plains Cree independent indicative examples. Morphological breakdowns are deliberately omitted where the source segmentation has not been separately established. The Woodland Cree examples remain unpopulated until confirmed against TH-specific references.

Learning notes

1 = first-person singular, 2 = second-person singular, 3 = third-person proximate and 3′ = third-person obviative. First/second-person local interactions have their own agreement patterns and should not be guessed from 1→3 or 3→1.

Suggested reference for additional source checking: Jean L. Okimāsis, Cree: Language of the Plains; consult verb paradigms and relevant page references before describing these as fully source-verified TH forms. Also consult Solomon Ratt's dialect-specific works.
