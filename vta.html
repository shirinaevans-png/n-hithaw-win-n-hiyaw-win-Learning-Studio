Animate grammatical object

VTA

Transitive animate

A verb takes an object grammatically classified as animate. Its form can reflect who acts on whom and singular/plural agreement.

Direction

Direct versus inverse

The same VTA stem can appear with different person and direction markers. Do not simply replace the final letter of an unrelated verb.

Compare a direct form meaning “I see someone” to an inverse form meaning “someone sees me.”

Animate and inanimate are Cree grammatical classifications. They do not always correspond to living versus non-living things in English. This lab uses curated examples rather than treating one conjugation pattern as universal.
