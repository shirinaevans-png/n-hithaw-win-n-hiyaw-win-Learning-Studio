How Cree Word Endings Work

Interactive morphology cards, root and stem analysis, lexical suffix patterns, VAI/VII classes, and a source-aware practice quiz.

What the learner is looking at

Cree is polysynthetic: a word can combine a lexical root or initial, a medial/final, preverbs, and person/number inflection. A recurring sequence of letters is not automatically one morpheme. Each card below distinguishes an attested dictionary entry from a proposed segmentation or spelling pattern.

Root

Core lexical element: pakwân- “canvas” (from the ALTLab entry supplied by the learner).

Stem

A complete base taking inflection; the citation form may contain more than one root/final.

Final

Derivational element, often contributing meaning or verb class; compare dwelling-related -kamik.

Inflection

Marks person, number, order, etc. In some independent VAI forms, final -w marks third person; do not assume every final w has that analysis.
