# Source-grounded grammar architecture

This release provides selectable person relationships, independent/conjunct/imperative order and obviative reference, plus exact-match checking against **all** source records. It does **not** certify unattested forms, and the selectable grid is not a claim of complete conjugation coverage.

## Papers analyzed (source interpretations)

- Wolvengrey, *Inversion and the absence of grammatical relations in Plains Cree* (2005): VAI, VII, VTI, VTA classes, bound person indexing and direct–inverse. Person roles cannot be reduced to English subject–object slots.
- Wolvengrey, *Prospective Aspect in the Western Dialects of Cree* (2006): distinguish wî- prospective aspect from ta-/ka- future. A future tense menu must not conflate them.
- Harrigan et al., *Learning from the computational modelling of Plains Cree verbs* (2017): finite-state morphological generation and analysis require lexicon, inflectional rules and morphophonological alternations.
- Snoek et al., *Modeling the Noun Morphology of Plains Cree* (2014): animate/inanimate number, possession, obviation, locative, diminutives, and stem alternations interact.
- Eun Bi Kang, *Morphological variation in Cree inflection*: conjunct morphology and third-person pluralizers vary across dialects; avoid reusing one dialect's endings.
- Henke, *Acquiring Relational Verb Inflection in Northern East Cree* (2026): relational inflection is distinct and should not be generalized to the TH/Y dialect modules.
- Will Oxford, *Algonquian Languages*: Cree dialect continuum and inflectional typology.
- James Evans, *Cree Syllabic Hymn Book* facsimile: historical writing-system context, not a modern normative chart.

## What remains

For full inflection of every dictionary lemma we require a verified stem lexicon with subclass and dialect, full independent/conjunct/imperative paradigms, allomorphy, VTA local/direction and obviation conditions, plus speaker review. The model can then be compiled into a finite-state transducer. Research papers do not provide a verified full lexicon or complete inflection inventory for 1,923 entries.
