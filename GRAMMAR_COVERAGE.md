# Conjugation coverage and limits

This package exposes every attested person/relationship in the supplied 64-record conjugation database. A matrix exposes missing slots, rather than manufacturing forms. It supports independent per-dialect selection, full dictionary verb search and exact-match validation of recorded outputs.

It does **not** implement an exhaustive Plains Cree or Woodland Cree morphological transducer. The supplied lexicon alone cannot certify stems, person-number inflections, conjunct/independent orders, obviation or allomorphy. In particular, further obviative and absentative are included only in conceptual guidance, not guessed paradigms. The source material and speaker review must support new forms before certification.

Both introductions now include Roman orthography inventories, vowel length and illustrative syllabic series. No rights-restricted textbook pages were included.
