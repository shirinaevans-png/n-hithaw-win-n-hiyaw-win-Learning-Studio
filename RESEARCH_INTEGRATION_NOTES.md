# Source-based additions and scope

This release adds a small set of attested Plains Cree forms and connects sixteen VAI agreement examples (four stems × four grammatical persons including obviative) to the conjugation checker. Existing vocabulary entries are reused instead of duplicated. The cited forms come from Solomon Ratt, *mâci-nêhiyawêwin: Beginning Cree* (2016), chapter 1, pp. 11–13. The conjugation records intentionally use the **whole attested form** when affix boundaries are not verified. Whole-form matching is not a productive inflection rule.

Other uploaded linguistic papers inform the design without contributing a wholesale reproduced lexicon: Wolvengrey (2005) direct/inverse and person marking; Wolvengrey (2006) prospective `wî-` versus future `ta-/ka-`; Harrigan et al. (2017) finite-state analysis/generation and morphophonology; Snoek et al. (2014) noun possession, plurals, locative and obviation; Eun Bi Kang (2017) conjunct dialectal variation; Henke (2026) Northern East Cree relational inflection (not transferable to TH/Y); Oxford, *Algonquian Languages*, comparative linguistic background; Evans, *Cree Syllabic Hymn Book*, historical syllabics only, not a modern alphabet authority.

Ratt (2022), *âhkami-nêhiyawêtân*, offers expanded VAI/VTI/VTA instructional paradigms but is copyrighted; this package does not reproduce its full charts. Likewise, no text or images from the copyrighted illustrated dictionaries or story readers are redistributed.

The existing master dictionary (1,923 records before this update) already includes extensive Ratt-derived vocabulary; public reuse rights should be checked with the publisher before publishing the package. No learner uploads are needed.
