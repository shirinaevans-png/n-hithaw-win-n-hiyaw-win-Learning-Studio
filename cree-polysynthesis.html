How Polysynthesis Works in Cree

Explore how meaningful parts combine inside Cree words.

1. What is polysynthesis?

In a polysynthetic language, a single word can contain multiple meaningful grammatical elements. Cree verbs may express information about participants, the action, and how the event unfolds. Polysynthesis is about word structure; SVO, SOV and other patterns describe sentence structure.
