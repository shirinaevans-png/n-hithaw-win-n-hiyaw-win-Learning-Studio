Language and source notes

This is a practice tool, not a certified numeral translator. Written examples vary by dialect, community and orthographic convention. The Woods Cree reference is not specifically a Lac La Ronge community-verified paradigm. Do not treat a set of separately displayed components as a grammatically verified spoken compound. Syllabics are omitted rather than automatically transliterating uncertain combinations.

Sources: Woods Cree number table; Plains Cree number table; Cree Language Project: Numbers in Cree; SICC Woodland Cree (TH) counting flashcards. Speaker review is recommended before publishing as an authoritative Lac La Ronge teaching resource.
