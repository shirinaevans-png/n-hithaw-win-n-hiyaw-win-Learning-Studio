Some Cree words change depending on whether you are talking about an animate or inanimate person or thing. Others help you ask questions, point to something, give directions, or connect thoughts. Explore the words below, then practise. Words without a documented dialect label remain in Source dialect not specified; they are not automatically assigned to TH or Y. On this page, demonstratives and pronouns are also colour-shaded to show animate forms (teal) and inanimate forms (gold) when that distinction is given in the source.

Source references are taken from the supplied Cree vocabulary database. The English gloss and grammatical class are transcribed from those records.
