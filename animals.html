Animal Flashcards

nîhithawîwin (TH) · nêhiyawêwin (Y)

Tap an animal to flip the card and reveal its Cree word. Choose the dialect before practising. Only dictionary-supported forms are shown as confirmed; other entries remain labelled for verification.

Illustrations are being added in stages. Six cards have artwork; others show an illustration-pending symbol. Audio plays only when a speaker recording has been added with permission. Published dictionary entries and dialect labels should be reviewed with fluent speakers. ALTLab itwêwina supports Plains Cree research.
