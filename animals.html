Animal Flashcard Studio

Practise animal vocabulary in Plains Cree (Y) and Woodland Cree (TH). Published dictionary words are identified separately from entries awaiting verification. Illustrated sample cards are included; remaining pictures can be added later.

Audio: Upload speaker recordings as MP3 files and add their relative paths to animals-data.js (audio_y / audio_th). Audio controls appear only when recordings exist. The dictionary links may include speaker recordings on the original site. Do not copy third-party audio without permission.

Sources: ALTLab itwêwina (Plains Cree dictionary, based on Wolvengrey and partner sources). TH forms require checking against Woods Cree sources and Lac La Ronge speakers.
