2. How noun class changes the verb

Cree often expresses English adjectives as descriptive verbs. You do not attach an English-style adjective to every noun. Instead the descriptive verb must select the right grammatical class.

4. What about VAI, VII, VTA, and VTI?

VAI · Animate intransitive

One grammatical participant, an animate subject. E.g., an animate thing is red.

VII · Inanimate intransitive

One grammatical participant, an inanimate subject. E.g., an inanimate thing is red.

VTA · Transitive animate

The verb selects an animate object or goal. Example: seeing a grammatically animate rock.

VTI · Transitive inanimate

The verb selects an inanimate object or goal. Example: seeing a shoe.

Transitive verb classes are not determined by the English meaning alone. The verb's grammatical argument structure matters, and particular verbs may involve additional derivational or inflectional changes.
