The Learning Studio contains 5,816 searchable entries. For many more Plains Cree dictionary entries and generated inflectional forms, use the research resources below. These links open the source projects; they do not copy their full databases into this website.

Plains Cree (Y) — itwêwina

The ALTLab intelligent Plains Cree dictionary provides Cree ↔ English search, grammatical labels, syllabics, and analyses and paradigms of inflected words. It is the best external source for extensive Plains Cree lookup.

Research basis

Solomon Ratt, mâci-nêhiyawêwin (2016) and âhkami-nêhiyawêtân (2022), support the Plains Cree learning examples; his Woodland Cree work informs the separate TH-dialect sections. The uploaded papers by Wolvengrey, Harrigan and collaborators, Snoek and collaborators, and Oxford explain morphological analysis, inversion, noun structure and Cree dialect differences.

Local entry counts refer to entries packaged with this site. The much larger itwêwina lexical collection remains hosted and maintained by ALTLab; the site's local count does not include it.
