Syllabics.net. “Indigenous Syllabics Charts and Transliteration Tools.” Accessed October 20, 2026. https://www.syllabics.net/.

This site provides dialect-specific syllabics charts, browser-based transliteration, worksheets and practice, including Plains, Woods, Swampy, Moose and East Cree. The tool converts written forms; it does not provide Cree dictionary translations.
