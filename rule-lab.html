How to interpret the output

Recorded: The exact word occurs in your source records for the selected analysis.

Unverified analogy: The tool combines attested prefix and suffix segments with a different stem. It does not establish that the result is grammatically correct.

Unavailable: The database lacks the corresponding pattern, so the tool declines to generate a form.

Conjunct order, imperative, obviation, inverse alignment and preverb rules are not automatically extrapolated from an unrelated paradigm.
