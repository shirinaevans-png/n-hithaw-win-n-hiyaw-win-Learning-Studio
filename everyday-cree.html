Learn everyday vocabulary and practise published Cree sentences. This is a source-grounded teaching draft: words from a dictionary are presented separately from complete attested sentences, and examples from other varieties should be checked for Lac La Ronge use.

Bundle Two — My Bedroom & Getting Dressed

Source limitation: No complete Lac La Ronge TH translations of the proposed first-person clothing statements could be verified in the sources inspected. Accordingly, no invented Cree sentences appear in this bundle.

Sourced word bank

mitâs — pants

Activity: Word identification, matching and listening. Sentence practice should be added only after full forms such as “I get dressed” and “I like wearing sweatpants” are checked with the Woodland Cree Conversation Manual or speaker.

Age & Birthdays

Ask someone's age, share your age, ask when their birthday is, and talk about birthday dates. These examples are drawn from published Plains Cree (Y) learning materials. Lac La Ronge Woodland Cree (TH) versions have not yet been independently confirmed.

EnglishCree (Plains Y, as published)Source
How old are you?tānitahtopiponēyan?Cree: Language of the Plains, Session 10
I am 23 years old.nīsitanaw nistosāp nitispīhtisīn.Cree: Language of the Plains, Session 10
When is your birthday?tānispīhk kiya kā-tipiskaman?Cree: Language of the Plains, Session 10
On June 9th.kēkā-mitātaht akimihci pāskwāhihowi-pīsim.Cree: Language of the Plains, Session 10
Today is my birthday!anohc nitipiskên!mâci-nêhiyawêwin, Dialogue Five
I am twenty-one years old.nîsitanaw pêyakosâp nititahtopinonân.mâci-nêhiyawêwin, Dialogue Five

Dialect note: The forms above retain the spelling and grammatical construction of their respective published sources. Do not treat these examples as verified TH dialect translations.
