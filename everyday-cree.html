Bundle One — In the Home

Outcome: Ask where someone lives and state where you live.

EnglishCreeSource
Where do you live now?tânitê mêkwâc kiwîkin?Ratt, Dialogue 2
I live in Regina now.oskana kâ-asastêki mêkwâc niwîkin.Ratt, Dialogue 2
I am from Regina; I also live there now.oskana kâ-asastêki ohci niya, êkota mina mêkwâc niwîkin.Ratt, Dialogue 1

Bundle Five — Introductions & Conversation

Outcome: Introduce yourself, ask about names, background and community.

EnglishCreeSource
Hello / How are you?tânisi.Ratt, Dialogue 1
Fine. How about you?namôya nânitaw. kiya mâka.Ratt, Dialogue 1
The same. My name is Solomon.pêyakwan. Solomon nitisiyihkâson.Ratt, Dialogue 1
What is your name?tânisi kitisiyihkâson?Ratt, Dialogue 2
Where are you from originally?tânitê ohci kiya kayahtê?Ratt, Dialogue 2
